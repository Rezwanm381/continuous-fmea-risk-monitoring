# Methodological sources

Only sources directly used by the project are listed. `Source_Claim` records what the source supports; `Project_Adaptation` records the bounded design choice made here. An adaptation is not standards compliance, certification, or endorsement.

| Source_Claim | Source | Project_Adaptation | Direct_or_Adapted | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FMEA identifies failure modes/effects/causes and supports treatment prioritization | [IEC 60812:2018 official abstract](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/26359) | Structures the governed static FMEA register | Direct summary | No standards-compliance claim; full standard was not reproduced |
| A condition-monitoring programme follows general procedures applicable to machines | [ISO 17359:2018 official abstract](https://www.iso.org/standard/71194.html) | Grounds the separate condition-observation layer | Direct summary | Current edition confirmed in 2023; no certification claim |
| Condition-monitoring terminology has a standardized vocabulary | [ISO 13372:2012 official abstract](https://www.iso.org/standard/52256.html) | Provides terminology context | Direct summary | No standards-compliance claim |
| Condition-based maintenance connects condition data acquisition, processing, diagnostics/prognostics, and maintenance decisions | [Jardine, Lin & Banjevic, 2006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ymssp.2005.09.012) | Supports the separated condition-observation and decision-support architecture | Architecture adapted | Does not standardize this prototype's condition-informed FMEA rule |
| Manufacturing PHM requires verification, validation, and standards before operational reliance | [NIST review, Vogl, Weiss & Helu, 2016](https://www.nist.gov/publications/review-diagnostic-and-prognostic-capabilities-and-best-practices-manufacturing) | Supports prototype/validation claim boundaries | Direct summary | No field-validity inference is made from benchmark performance |
| Traditional RPN has ordinal-scale, discontinuity, duplicate-value, and sensitivity limitations | [Bowles, 2004/2005](https://doi.org/10.17764/jiet.47.1.y576m26127157313) | Keeps RPN secondary rather than predictive or decisive | Direct summary | Does not imply that one alternative method is mandatory |
| FMEA output validity and multiplication of ordinal S/O/D warrant caution | [Shebl et al., 2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3405478/) | Supports limitations language | Direct summary | Healthcare context; only mathematical/construct critique is generalized |
| FD001 contains per-engine multivariate trajectories, settings, sensors, one condition/mode, and official RUL truth | [NASA C-MAPSS metadata](https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/cmapss-jet-engine-simulated-data) | Dataset/target/split design | Direct | NASA describes the dataset as simulated and lists no license |
| The NASA PCoE repository provides the official archive and citation | [NASA PCoE repository](https://www.nasa.gov/intelligent-systems-division/discovery-and-systems-health/pcoe/pcoe-data-set-repository/) | Acquisition, attribution, provenance | Direct | Raw redistribution remains unresolved |
| C-MAPSS degradation data were created for prognostic algorithm development | [Saxena et al., 2008](https://doi.org/10.1109/PHM.2008.4711414) | Interprets the benchmark as simulated run-to-failure evidence | Direct/adapted | Does not establish field deployment |
| GroupKFold keeps each group out of paired training/validation folds | [scikit-learn GroupKFold](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.model_selection.GroupKFold.html) | Uses engine ID as the group | Direct mechanism; engine mapping adapted | Relative engine clocks are not one global time series |
| Learned preprocessing should not see held-out data | [scikit-learn leakage guidance](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html#data-leakage) | Pipelines fit imputation/scaling inside training folds | Direct | Applies to every learned transform; fixed constant-field exclusions are not learned |
| Precision-recall evaluation is useful under class imbalance | [scikit-learn precision-recall guide](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/auto_examples/model_selection/plot_precision_recall.html) | Makes average precision/PR-AUC primary | Direct | ROC-AUC remains secondary |
| Reliability curves and proper probability scores assess probability quality | [scikit-learn calibration guide](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/calibration.html) | Uses Brier score and calibration plots; gates sigmoid recalibration | Direct | Brier reflects calibration and resolution, so the curve is also inspected |
| Condition monitoring can support planned intervention before functional failure | [NASA maintenance guidance](https://nodis3.gsfc.nasa.gov/displayDir.cfm?Internal_ID=N_PR_8831_002F_&page_name=Chapter7) | Supports cautious decision-support framing | Adapted | No claim of achieved operational benefit |
| UCI Hydraulic Systems has measured 60-second rig cycles and CC BY 4.0 rights | [UCI dataset 447](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/447/condition+monitoring+of+hydraulic+systems) | Alternative-dataset assessment | Direct | Not selected for the future-event task |
| MetroPT-3 is a real timestamped APU stream with 15 signals and CC BY 4.0 rights | [UCI dataset 791](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/791/metropt+3+) | Alternative-dataset assessment | Direct | One apparent asset/four reported event intervals limits validation |

The sources support a two-layer, human-reviewed decision prototype. They do not support predicting RPN, multiplying probability into a "dynamic RPN," interpreting importance causally, or claiming deployment, failure prevention, safety certification, or production-fleet calibration.
