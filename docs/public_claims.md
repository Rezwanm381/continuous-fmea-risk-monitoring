# Public claim boundaries

These boundaries govern future portfolio text, captions, repository descriptions, and interviews. They do not authorize publication.

## SAFE_PUBLIC_CLAIMS

- “The historical predictive implementation was invalidated because its target was constructed from predictor-side quantities.”
- “The rebuilt prototype predicts a separate 30-cycle future degradation event on NASA C-MAPSS FD001.”
- “Grouped engine-level validation, fold-local preprocessing, and trailing-only features were used to control asset and temporal leakage.”
- “The selected Logistic Regression achieved PR-AUC approximately 0.905 on the documented official benchmark test construction.”
- “Nested sigmoid calibration was evaluated on grouped development data and rejected because its Brier improvement did not meet the predefined gate.”
- “Among 25 eligible official-test engines, 16 received a qualifying warning; the estimate has substantial small-sample and follow-up uncertainty.”
- “Under the defined illustrative scenario policies, the condition-informed policy showed higher warning coverage and lower row-alert burden.”
- “Static S/O/D/RPN remain unchanged; model output affects only a separate urgency layer.”

Every metric claim must retain its benchmark, retrospective, and eligibility context.

## UNSUPPORTED_OR_PROHIBITED_CLAIMS

- “The model predicts real turbofan failures.”
- “The model prevents failures or reduces maintenance cost.”
- “Machine learning improves FMEA.”
- “Condition-informed FMEA is superior to traditional FMEA.”
- “The model has 99.7% accuracy.”
- “The 80% threshold guarantees 80% test or field recall.”
- “The model is perfectly calibrated.”
- “NASA validates or endorses this maintenance method.”
- “IEC 60812 certifies this implementation.”
- “The condition bands are industrial or standards-based thresholds.”
- “This is production ready, field validated, safety certified, or a deployed maintenance system.”

## Required qualifiers

- PR-AUC is the primary headline; high ROC-AUC requires benchmark-structure context.
- `64%` must appear as `16/25 eligible engines` with the approximate Wilson interval `44.5%–79.8%` when space permits.
- Static-versus-condition findings must say `SCENARIO_POLICY_ILLUSTRATION` and disclose that only development alert-state rows were nearly matched.
- FMEA rows and probability bands must remain labeled as scenario assumptions pending domain review.
- Data provenance is `PUBLIC_VERIFIED`; redistribution is `VERIFY_BEFORE_PUBLICATION`.
